Frequently asked questions

How do I add clothes to my wardrobe?
From the Home or Wardrobe tab, tap "Add Items," then snap a photo or choose one from your library. Fill in the category, color, and any other details — Wardrobe AI saves it to your digital closet.

How does Wardrobe AI build outfit suggestions?
Open the "Find Outfit" tab, tell it the occasion, time of day, and weather, and it builds a complete look using only clothes already in your wardrobe.

How do I change the app's language?
Go to Profile → Language, and choose English, French, Spanish, or Japanese — or leave it on System Default to follow your phone's language.

How do I manage or cancel my Premium subscription?
Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Go to your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or use Profile → Manage Subscription inside the app, to change or cancel your plan at any time.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your wardrobe, saved looks, and account data — this action cannot be undone.
